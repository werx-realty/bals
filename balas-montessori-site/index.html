The purpose of Montessori

Not filling minds — nurturing a love of learning.

Dr. Maria Montessori believed no one is educated by another person — a child must do it themselves. A truly educated person keeps learning long after the school years, because they're moved by their own curiosity and love of knowledge.

In our classroom, each child experiences the excitement of learning by their own choice, and perfects their natural tools for learning so their ability is at its best in every future learning situation.

“The goal of early childhood education is to cultivate the child's own natural desire to learn.”— Dr. Maria Montessori

Montessori curriculum

Seven areas, one joyful classroom.

Each area is introduced simply — even for our youngest — and grows richer and more complex as your child matures.

🧺

Practical Life

Pouring, buttoning, caring for self and classroom — the roots of focus and independence.

🔷

Sensorial

Hands-on materials that refine the senses: size, color, shape, sound and texture.

🔤

Language

Sounds, sandpaper letters, early reading and writing at each child's pace.

🔢

Mathematics

Golden beads and number rods turn abstract ideas into something you can hold.

Cultural Studies & Sciences

Geography, nature, animals and the world around us — sparking wonder and big questions.

🤝

Social Behavior & Physical Activity

Grace and courtesy, kindness, teamwork and plenty of movement — growing the whole child, not just the mind.

Tuition & admission policy

I understand there will be at least 30 days' notice of any rate change. Prices shown are full-day prices; half-day prices are available on request. A $150 non-refundable registration fee applies to new students.

Trial period. During your child's first 2 weeks we will evaluate your child in many areas to determine if this program is the right fit. If it is not, your tuition will be refunded on a prorated basis.

Changes & absences. If you enter, leave, or change programs after the 1st day of school, tuition will be prorated accordingly. Discounts can be made for illness-related absences of more than 10 consecutive days — please discuss these with Mrs. Balas. To discontinue the program for any reason, a 30-day written notice is required, and you are financially obligated, without exception, for that 30-day period.

Payment. Tuition is due on the 1st of each month. A $40 late charge is added for tuition received after 5 PM on the 1st, no exceptions, and a 5% penalty accrues daily until tuition is current. Accounts 30 days past due will be turned over to our collection agency, H.P. Sears of Bakersfield, CA. If payment is not made by the 20th, a leave of absence will begin until tuition is current. If payment or satisfactory arrangements are not made by the 1st of the following month, your child's space may be filled from the waiting list.

Licensing. The Department of Social Services, our licensing agency, has authority to interview children or staff and to inspect and audit child or facility records without prior consent; to observe the physical condition of any child, including conditions indicating neglect, abuse, or inappropriate placement; and to have children examined by a licensed medical professional. Provisions will be made for private interviews with any child if the need arises.
